# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Reel Rod Squad project a
welcoming, respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Welcome constructive feedback and differing viewpoints.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.
- Respect the privacy and safety of junior members and all club participants.
- Do not post personal information, passwords, access tokens or private club
  records.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, abusive language, doxxing, and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainers through
the contact method listed on the repository's GitHub page. Do not include
sensitive personal information in public issues.

Maintainers will review reports and take appropriate action, which may include
editing or removing content, closing discussions, or restricting participation.
